Loading data

Use the file or folder buttons, or drop files or folders anywhere in the window. CT and MR series: uncompressed, deflated, RLE, JPEG baseline and JPEG lossless. JPEG 2000 and JPEG-LS are not supported. NIfTI volumes (.nii, .nii.gz) and ZIP files of DICOM images load too.

Demo scan

A full head-and-neck CT angiography in DICOM, the sample scan of Kitware's open-source VolView viewer, downloaded from data.kitware.com (about 80 MB). It is kept in the browser after the first download. Without internet a synthetic phantom is shown instead.
